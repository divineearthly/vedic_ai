=== VEDIC WISDOM ===

BRAHMAN: The ultimate reality, source of all existence. Eternal, infinite, blissful consciousness.

ATMAN: The individual self, identical with Brahman. Pure consciousness within all beings.

DHARMA: Righteous duty, truth, law of being. The path that upholds cosmic order.

KARMA: Action and its consequences. Law of cause and effect in consciousness.

MOKSHA: Liberation from cycle of rebirth. Freedom realized through self-knowledge.

VEDAS: Four sacred texts - Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva. Source of eternal wisdom.

UPANISHADS: Philosophical texts, essence of Vedas. Teach identity of Atman and Brahman.

YOGA: Union of individual consciousness with cosmic consciousness. Paths: Karma, Bhakti, Jnana, Raja.

MANTRA: Sacred sound vibration. Tool for focusing and elevating consciousness.
